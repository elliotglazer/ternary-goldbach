# Computational certification options, 12 November 2026

The present evidence does not establish that a standard-foundation formal proof
of ternary Goldbach is unviable. It establishes a serious cost problem for the
current stock-Lean certificate representation. A verified fast evaluator is a
substantive alternative, and Candle already demonstrates that architecture.
No complete TG checker or cross-system translation is claimed. A complete small
finite Goldbach interval is now checked in both Lean and HOL4; its measured
costs are recorded below. A new conditional model of complete proposed
implementations is in forecast-audit-20261113/END_TO_END_MODEL.md. Its explicit
unmeasured assumptions must remain visible when quoting any total.

The verified-evaluator approach represents a whole checking algorithm, uses
efficient arithmetic internally, and returns a theorem justified by a proved
soundness result. It need not serialize every elementary arithmetic proof step.
This differs from adding a new number-theoretic axiom. However, proving an
interpreter correct in Lean does not itself allow arbitrary external execution
to discharge a Lean premise. Stock Lean must still check evidence of this run,
or its trusted evaluation implementation must change. A proof in Candle cannot
simply be imported as a three-axiom Lean theorem without such a bridge.

and 89858217607168 time butterflies, totaling 218799609396458. If all of those

roots, other point products, the large-conductor branch, principal zeros,

remain additional. The older full models used speculative arithmetic expansion
factors; they are not lower bounds on efficient implementations.

## What would change feasibility

The strongest current alternative is a verified evaluator that certifies an
entire block and returns a short mathematical conclusion. A zero-count theorem
or a theorem covering an interval of Goldbach targets need not contain all the
intermediate numerical values in its statement. The checker can consume them
internally, prove the aggregate result and discard intermediate theorem values.
This requires exact input binding, a proved checker-to-mathematics implication
and an actual justified execution. A success log alone is not that evidence.

Three deliverables must be distinguished. An unchanged stock-Lean proof must
still replay in that kernel. A modified evaluator proved sound for Lean's logic
could preserve the logical axioms while changing the trusted implementation and
the accepted proof format. A Candle/HOL theorem uses a different existing
foundation and does not automatically become a Lean theorem. None of these
options requires assuming PlattGRH as a new mathematical axiom; none currently
supplies the full TG computation in this project.

For finite prime coverage, the earlier native packed-bitset prototype provides
a separate optimization lead. Its audited 0.340 ns/even warm-data throughput
and conditional 50–300 core-year integrated-checker design are documented in
../mm0-efficient/SCOPE.md and AUDIT.md. That design is a prospective specialized
verified checker, not an ordinary MMB result. Its source-primality, scheduling,
implementation proof and full-range workload still require validation. Do not
combine this projection and the spectral fixture projection into a claimed
end-to-end total: the spectral model omits major obligations, and neither rate
has been established for a completed verifier.

That 4e18 binary task is also not mandatory for the currently proposed TG plan.
The older integrated finite model chooses B=1e16 for Lean and B=2e16 for ordinary
MM0, trading a smaller binary computation against a much denser prime ladder.
Its Lean total is conditionally 6/18/477 core-years across three scenarios,
including generation and checking. Those figures are retained historical
assumptions, not new measurements or validated upper bounds. In particular the
special-rung 55-microsecond checking claim was not replicated, and complete
source-prime, fallback and range-assembly costs were budgeted rather than built.
Any new end-to-end model must re-optimize this tradeoff for its actual backend,
instead of silently requiring binary Goldbach through 4e18. See
../qrh-blueprint/estimates-20261111/FINITE_COST_ESTIMATE.md.

The current kernels have a relevant asymmetry. Lean 4.35.0 directly reduces
Nat.land, Nat.lor, Nat.xor and the two shifts using native literal operations.
The earlier wide-bitset work already exploited this; see
../formal-goldbach-bench/architecture.md and its audited measurements. HOL4's
current Compute.sml primitive set has arithmetic but no bitwise operator, and
cv_primScript.sml implements word OR/AND/XOR by recursive per-bit arithmetic.
Consequently the spectral arithmetic comparison cannot establish the cost of
packed prime coverage in stock cv_compute. A separate benchmark or a proved
extension is needed. Do not assume that a good high-precision arithmetic backend
automatically preserves the native bitset throughput.
