# Dynamic completion phases

At tick j the two global parity factors are the unit gamma phases from large-coordinate-sources, including exp(−it log π/2). Each conductor supplies one rounded unit step exp(−5i log(q)/128). State starts at (2^126,0). Each tick multiplies the two actual gamma centers by the same p126 state and rounds directly to p105, records the strict real/imag signs, then advances the p126 state. The result is in physical parity order even, odd. Within each parity the two low bits encode the real sign, followed by two bits for the imaginary sign. Codes 0, 1, 2 denote unresolved, negative and positive. For the primitive q10004 flattening even row index selects odd parity; for prime q131071 even k selects even parity. A zero sign is passed to a fallback.

The maximum-guard native audit in prepare.py covers all 84,881 ticks at q10004 and 6,378 ticks at q131071. It compares all 182,518 computed complex factors to direct independently evaluated combined normalized angles at 208-bit precision. Errors stay below 0.710 and 0.707 p105 grid units respectively. The two ambiguous coordinates in each conductor are the imaginary parts of the even and odd phases at tick zero. native.json records mask digests and bounds. All inputs to the expensive gamma step can be shared by tick across conductors, while the q-dependent state persists throughout a single conductor. The p126 gamma centers here are generated by Arb; a p105 gamma cache alone does not satisfy this working-precision assumption.

DynamicPrice checked packed gamma input, dynamic mask and output q state for 16 tiles at both ends of the two conductors. Its 465 and 490 ticks total 955. DynamicCarry checked the exact intervening q-state runs of 84,416 and 5,888 updates so all high starting states have an unbroken chain to the unit seed. The two carry-only runs used 0.567735 and 0.060277 CPU seconds. The stock masks and state checks assume their actual rounded elementary inputs. Full native checks include the middle masks; stock middle-mask receipts were sampled only at the two ends.
