# Two character lanes in one positive time transform

The zero-extra-node schedule contains 18,477,108 primitive character lanes at 7,499 conductors. Pair adjacent lanes within each conductor. This gives 9,236,019 pairs and 5,070 singletons. All members of each pair have the same length, precision, support, target prefix and tilt. Each retains its original character phase, source coefficients, sign threshold and analytic error budget. The counts in counts.json group pairs by length and precision. They price one coarse grid.

## Packing and error

Write A and D for the two exact arrays of stored Hermitian coefficient centers. Their positive unnormalized DFTs U and V are real. The positive DFT of C=A+iD equals U+iV. At DC copy the two real centers into their respective coordinates. For each positive j with A_j=x+iy and D_j=v+iw the two occupied coefficients are (x-w)+i(y+v) and (x+w)+i(v-y). This costs four real additions per j. All remaining slots are zero. Taking either coordinate after the common exact shift by N gives that lane's raw sample. The negative packed slot generally differs from the conjugate of the positive packed slot.

Let B be the existing row's coefficient norm bound, k=log2 N and u=2^-p for its recorded raw precision p. Every exact normalized intermediate has norm at most 2B by convexity. Assemble packed inputs exactly on their common source grid, then round once to p. This gives initial norm error at most u, including when the source is finer. Budget at most 2J+1 complex conversions per pair. They are exact when all source centers already lie on the time grid. Each time root and each named rounding must have norm error at most u. A nearest-grid complex rounding on grid 2^-p satisfies this.

For exact even and odd children X,Y, both with norm ≤2B, the normalized outputs are (X ± wY)/2. The product uses a rounded unit root and one product rounding. Each output rounds once after the exact halving. A uniform input error e propagates to at most (1+u/2)e+(B+3/2)u; (B+2)u is sufficient for the additive term. This also bounds the retained outputs of the pruned graph, with normalization preserved along single-child paths. With ku≤1/2 the geometric amplification stays below two, yielding raw coordinate error ≤N[2+k(2B+4)]u. The original sign allowance is eps/(64H)=7 eps N Delta/2816. pair.py checks the strict comparison for every row with rational arithmetic and the original row-specific B, eps and p. The source approximation remains covered by the existing analytic comparison. This calculation concerns the coarse base arrays. Off-grid phase preparation would require its own input-error accounting.

The packed coefficient and normalized node registers require at most one additional magnitude bit compared with the corresponding B-based bounds, with the same rounding guard. The same doubling applies to the pre-halving sum and root-product bounds relative to their existing bounds. Exact source scratch must accommodate addition before conversion. Each exported coordinate keeps its original raw bound. No additional fractional bits are required on the 7,499 tested rows. The required pair traversal, scratch widths, packing, input conversions, shared node normalizations, output selection and root checks must be implemented to realize the savings.
