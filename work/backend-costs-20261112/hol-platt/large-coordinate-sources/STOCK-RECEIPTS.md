# Stock proof and gamma-phase receipts

PreDiagonal.remove_diagonal proves the Euclidean bound d*b+r+e for U−conj(c)D. The assumptions are |c|=1, |c−h|≤d, |U|≤b, |hU−V|≤r and |V−D|≤e. Its unit_unpost lemma gives the exact isometry. The stock export pd03 completed in 0.453 theory seconds.

The full gamma computation uses j=0..84880, a=0,1, and g=1/4+a/2+5ji/128. The gamma phase includes exp(−5ji log π/128). At 200-bit working precision, full_gamma_native.py computed both coordinates of all 169,762 factors with a unique strict p105 bin in 4.734375 CPU seconds. At 220 bits, stirling_panel.py --full independently reproduced all 339,524 signed integer centers in 12.5625 CPU seconds. The SHA-256 of the agreed canonical centers is fa8049c206d6b88762897952521831c9a77b302f3cc604bc19f5902fe5518ae0.

For the independent evaluation, put z=g+64 when Im(g)<64 and z=g otherwise. In each case Re(z)>0 and |z|≥64. Retain terms k=1..19 in the log-gamma Stirling expansion. The 20th-term error is bounded by 2^20 |B40| / (40·39·64^39) < 2^−170 since sec(arg(z)/2)^40 ≤ 2^20. Use B40=−261082718496449122051/13530. For shifted arguments subtract the phase of the rising factorial of length 64. Phase equivalence modulo 2π suffices after exponentiation. The generic complex remainder is the external analytic input.
