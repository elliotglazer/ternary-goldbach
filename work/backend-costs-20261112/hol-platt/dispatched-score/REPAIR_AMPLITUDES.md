# Sparse amplitude requests for repair windows

The stock HOL4 theorem [enclosed_amplitude](RepairAmplitudeScript.sml) bounds the real projection by the integer center and radius returned by `ball`. It uses four coefficient-coordinate enclosures of radius one and two source-coordinate enclosures of radius `e`. It does not require the completed imaginary coordinate to vanish. Given phase, factor and spectrum in p81 units, divide both center and radius by (2^{243}) to recover their physical amplitude units. Each stratum must use its actual product of grid scales. A later height-dependent normalization or interpolation requires a separate error bound. [Build receipt](amplitude02.log)

The center and radius are the two integers calculated inside the fallback sign detector. The fast branch of `Absorbed.collect` generally reads one coordinate before postchirp rotation. To emit a full amplitude there, apply the original `FastAxis.rotate2` to the requested row of `zs` with its matching root in `ws`, then compute `ball` with the matching phase, factor and coordinate radius. In the fallback branch a fused implementation can emit the two integers it just computed. The present timed sign function produces bit masks only. [Existing consumer](../large-terminal-signs/AbsorbedScript.sml)

An untrusted discovery pass may propose the union of the interpolation stencils, keyed by conductor, exact character label, physical height tick, grid precision and source version. The formal producer validates each requested lookup against the same inputs as the sign sweep. In a planned streaming pass it can tap only those rows while the raw transform output and root table are resident. An unexpected repair requested after eviction requires a checkpoint carrying the relevant inputs or a replay of the affected transform tile. Final count acceptance must account for any unresolved requests, including increased precision or additional windows.

The [timing script](measure_window_taps.py) compares all 2,301 projected p81 production coordinates at conductor 10,004 with the archived sign mask. It also reconstructs the full postchirp and score pair on six archived p24 and p28 conductor-131,071 tiles. Of 2,688 row instances, 2,686 score intervals exclude zero and agree with the fast mask. Two p24 intervals contain zero. The six native timings cover postchirp and projection on resident input arrays. Kernel receipt production, source generation, durable storage and repeated tile loads require additional timing. [Native results](window_taps.json)
