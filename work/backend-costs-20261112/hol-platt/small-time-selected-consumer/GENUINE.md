## Campaign safety of recombination

The source manifest SHA-256 is e767ec3b3e14f2a243837da85c811de5ed67ce701bdd08998c2329f9c687c24d. campaign_bound.py independently recalculates L, working precision, X, Y, and the actual field width for all 7,499 source rows, comprising 18,477,108 primitive characters. Every allocated width from 536 to 744 bits satisfies 4LXY < 2^(w−1). Suppose the same product pairs are retained in A, B and C, each input coordinate is at most X in absolute value, and each chirp coordinate is at most Y. Then each real term au−bv and imaginary term av+bu has absolute value at most 2XY. Each set of at most L contributing pairs gives a partial sum of absolute value at most 2LXY. Zero, a partial carry, a completed coefficient, or a discarded middle-window piece all obey this bound when they consist of such matching pairs. Disjoint terms in adjacent diagonal contributions ensure that the total for any eventual coefficient still has at most L pairs. With R=2^w every original recombined coefficient therefore has absolute value less than R/2. Biasing every digit by R/2 puts it in [0,R), and the complete biased T-digit low integer is in [0,R^T). The earlier stock FusedCorrect theorems now give exactly the combined upper carries. This finite width check depends on the upstream analytic input envelope, matching channel support, and original convolution indexing.
