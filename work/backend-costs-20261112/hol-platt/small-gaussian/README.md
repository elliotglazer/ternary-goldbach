# Gaussian recurrence on the small conductors

The shorter frequency schedule calls for 2,285,390,500,061 rounded complex multiplications. The HOL4 sample prices the recurrence and accumulation into the even and odd residue tables at **1,687 projected core-hours**. The between-draw standard error is 15 hours. The three timing repetitions project to 1,699, 1,693 and 1,735 hours. This is a price for arithmetic inside the evaluator, where the next operation can consume the table directly. [Sample and timing calculation](costs.json) · [HOL4 run](bench01.log)

For each frequency the starting value is a dyadic approximation to the Gaussian base. If its mathematical value is \(q\), the two recurrences step through \(q^{n^2}\) and \(q^{2n+1}\) using a fixed increment \(q^2\) for the odd powers. The program accumulates \(q^{n^2}\) for even characters and \(nq^{n^2}\) for odd characters. It omits unused multiplications at the last index. Each integer complex multiplication uses three scalar products and rounds both coordinates toward zero once. [Executable theory](GaussScript.sml)

HOL4 proved for every positive integer scale that each rounded coordinate moves by less than one grid unit from the exact product, and that the complex multiplication preserves the closed unit disk on the scaled integer lattice. The second assertion uses the exact sum-of-squares identity for complex multiplication. These two results allow a per-gate Euclidean error allowance of \(2/S\) at scale \(S\). The proof that every analytic seed satisfies the input assumptions and the transfer of the recurrence bound to actual L-functions remain open. [General theorems](UnitBoundsScript.sml) · [Successful proof build](bounds05.log)

The integer ledger has 1,851,628,961 frequencies that require a recurrence. The sampler draws five frequencies with replacement in each of 13 length bands. A frequency's selection probability in its band is proportional to its retained length. For each draw, the estimator divides the paired full-minus-control time by that length and applies the total length in the band. The controls compare the same output tables and include a common first computation. Three paired runs supplied each median. One corrupted control value was rejected. The seed generator used 680-bit Arb and checked the seed radius and unit-disk condition on the sampled inputs. [Sampler](sample.py) · [Estimator](price.py)

Two full-vector equalities also passed. At conductor 3 the kernel accumulated 3,862 terms into three residue buckets. At conductor 10,000 it produced 5,303 separate buckets. The latter returns 21,212 integer coordinates. Each equality was checked against a separate four-product integer implementation. Exporting an ordinary typed table costs much more than keeping it in the evaluator. The conductor-10,000 downstream test measures the difference.
