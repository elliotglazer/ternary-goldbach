# Small-family guard costs

Each guard uses its frozen schedule of 7,499 conductors, with its own output count, transform length and minimum admissible theta. The time-product fold retains the 65,536-field tiles and four measured width classes. The grouped split reuses the two neighboring split rates for the unmeasured 680-bit class. The Gaussian fold recomputes the rational cutoff, coprime last term and retained frequency runs, and applies the earlier core-hour rate within each recurrence-length band.

The per-width product and split counts are in `time.json`. The number of accepted character transforms, gates, folded operations and retained Gaussian weights appear in `source.json`. Character work has two sensitivities. The same-length model transfers the baseline cost per FFT length, point or add operation to the new exact counts. A second fold reweights the original individual panel draws by their new density at the observed precision, exponent, fill and sign. Changed precision can move work outside the original panel population. The outside-population counts are given separately, and the second fold is diagnostic.

All four rows replace their zero-extra versions in the selected subtotal. These figures cover the original four arithmetic components. Additional setup, transform output, unmeasured operations and analytic certification retain their separate ledger treatment.
