The selected main+41 ledger has 269,921,342,390 whole cyclic slices of length at least 32. Three exact integer products per slice give 809,764,027,170 packed multiplications. ## Domain and traversal

The accepted combined-precision price ledger pins the structural manifest, per-conductor grids and 41-node guard join hashed in counts.json. All 125,400 rows joined by row number, conductor, totient and number of primitive characters. Odd conductors satisfy 10000<c<=132900; even conductors satisfy 10000<c<=265800 and 4 divides c. The separate 7,499 small conductors use a different algorithm. The later mean186 precision proposal is a different contract. The selected large grids actually used by the eligible slices span p80 through p105, including 4,801,108,455 slices on p81 in the main+41 scenario.

For each listed axis (n,q) in manifest order, slice multiplicity equals the number of previously retained labels times the product of following axis lengths. All n inputs are needed. The next axis receives n labels when q=0, or n-n/q otherwise. The convolution computes a complete cyclic transform and then passes only the projected outputs. Nearby axis reorderings and alternative chirp decompositions would change this accounting. At each conductor fold.py independently reconstitutes all five old semantic operation counts from that same root request and its slice multiplicity. The selected main totals recover 1,528,006,797 conductor-height nodes, 60,254,515,577,760 residue inputs and 36,468,561,640,066 final primitive outputs. Records in axis-histogram.csv.gz give length, grid, omission modulus, prefix capacity, byte lane, all input and retained output counts and slice multiplicities. The base scenario includes the guards. Adding extra times the last unweighted slice multiplicity produces each extension scenario.

The chosen engineering cutoff n>=32 leaves 27,469,537,910,200 short scalar slices on the existing path. The packed axes consume 47,891,544,124,008 input positions and retain 46,838,394,337,668 intermediate or final outputs. All eligible outer lengths are even. Odd lengths can occur inside the old recursive decomposition; charging them as additional packed transforms would duplicate work. The periodic odd-n chirp is (-1)^j exp(pi i j^2/n). Its sign factor restores period n, and the three chirps' signs cancel in the DFT identity.

## Computation, bounds and formal obligations

Fourteen length bins use each bin's maximum actual n, grid and input-capacity bound (these maxima can occur on different conductors). panel.py generates deterministic dense source integers and actual rounded chirps, caches the signed kernel pair, runs integer prechirps, packs both coordinates, executes three FLINT big-integer products, folds cyclically, unpacks and postchirps. Every bin was checked at four indices against a plain quadratic summation for those coefficients; timings exclude validation and cold chirp/kernel generation. Counts and medians use actual host wall samples, not a theoretical complexity model. No monotonic timing theorem makes the endpoints true upper bounds. The native-panel receipt preserves CPU samples as well; short Windows CPU samples can be zero at the process timer resolution.

With coordinate cap C=2 times the product of earlier full axis lengths times 16384, source grid S=2^p, the rounded prechirp has coordinate magnitude at most 2CS+2 and the kernel at most S. A signed cyclic coefficient has absolute value at most 2n(2CS+2)S. A byte-rounded signed lane above that magnitude prevents carries. The largest lane is 31 bytes; the largest single packed real operand is 4,119,652 bytes at n=132892, conductor 132893. Each ordinary product can occupy approximately twice an operand; recombination and signed temporaries add further words. Retaining every distinct kernel pair for (n,p,lane width) would use 52,736,516,520 bytes of raw packed payload over 21,230 keys. Process RSS also includes chirp lists, packing buffers, arithmetic scratch and proof objects. Streaming one key at a time or regrouping by width changes persistence and admission costs.

A new generic proof must establish correct cyclic packing and signed extraction, chirp identity, root enclosure and mode projection plus error propagation across the new axes. For input magnitude M and u=2^-p bounding complex root and rounding errors, one prechirp costs at most (M+1)u. The convolution with rounded unit chirps differs from the exact sum by at most n*((1+u)*(M+1)*u+M*u), before incoming source uncertainty. A final rounded postchirp multiplies that error bound by at most 1+u, adds n*M*u and one final u. The resulting bound must be composed with incoming source error and all later axes against the matched precision budget. Retaining the old grids in the price fold is a scenario pending this verification. Existing length-500 arithmetic measurements do not price full HOL admission, limb packing, extraction, decoding, or checking at the many observed sizes.
