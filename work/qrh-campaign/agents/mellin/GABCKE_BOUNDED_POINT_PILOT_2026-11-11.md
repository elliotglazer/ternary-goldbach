## Gabcke evaluator

At each exact rational t>=1500, the implementation certifies N=floor(sqrt(t/(2pi))) and sets a=sqrt(t/(2pi)), p=1-2(a-N). It encloses

\[
2\sum_{n=1}^N n^{-1/2}\cos(\widehat\theta-t\log n)
 +(-1)^{N-1}a^{-1/2}F(p),
\quad F(p)=\frac{\cos(\pi(p^2+3/4)/2)}{\cos(\pi p)}.
\]

The true-theta approximation is

\[
\widehat\theta=\theta_0+\frac1{48t}+\frac7{5760t^3}+\frac{31}{80640t^5},
\qquad \theta_0=\frac t2\log\frac{t}{2\pi}-\frac t2-\frac\pi8.
\]

It adds the audited analytic remainder .47 t^(-3/4) and phase error80/(3322t^7), alongside all interval rounding. The latter follows from the cosine Lipschitz bound, the normalized source theta remainder, and 2 sum_(n<=N)n^(-1/2)<80. It does not replace theta by theta0 without accounting for the difference.

When |u|<=1/4 for u=|p|-1/2, the evaluator uses

\[
F(1/2+u)=\frac{1+u}{2}
 \frac{\operatorname{sinc}(\pi u(1+u)/2)}{\operatorname{sinc}(\pi u)}.
\]

Evenness handles negative p. Each sinc evaluation uses the entire polynomial

\[
\sum_{n=0}^{24}\frac{(-1)^n v^{2n}}{(2n+1)!}
\]

and, for r>=|v| with r<1, adds a ball of radius

\[
\frac{r^{50}}{51!}\frac1{1-r^2/(52\cdot53)}.
\]

This bounds the omitted tail by a geometric majorant. The denominator sinc is explicitly certified greater than7/8 before division. Thus the near-removable cases do not divide two tiny sine values. Other p values use ordinary cosine division with a certified nonzero denominator. No derivative or truncated-jet claim is made in this K0 pilot.

## Independent reference expression

The reference is separately coded in the same pilot file and uses a different analytic formula and parameters from the frozen head/top evaluators. It directly evaluates zeta at s=1/2+it with N=ceil(t/2)+17, B=N+1, M=28:

\[
\zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{N}n^{-s}+\frac{B^{1-s}}{s-1}+\frac12B^{-s}
 +\sum_{k=1}^{M}\frac{B_{2k}}{(2k)!}(s)_{2k-1}B^{1-s-2k}+R.
\]

Bernoulli numbers are generated by exact Fraction recurrence. Positive real logarithms define all complex powers. The periodic-Bernoulli EM estimate used is

\[
|R|\le\frac{4|(s)_{2M}|B^{1-1/2-2M}}{6^{2M}(1/2+2M-1)}.
\]

This is a zeta error, with no missing or extra factor |s-1|. It is inserted into both Cartesian coordinates as a conservative square enclosure. The largest reference truncation bound among these points is below9.719e-26.

For the reference Hardy value, compute V=Re(exp(i theta0) zeta) and divide by an enclosure of cos(theta-theta0). The phase gap is enclosed by the three positive rational corrections above plus the source error interval +/-1/(3322t^7). The denominator is certified greater than.999. Actual Hardy realness gives Z=V/cos(theta-theta0). This reference does not use the Gabcke correction F or its remainder, and does not numerically evaluate Gamma.

Both evaluators share elementary Arb operations and the source phase bound, so this is a distinct-formula comparison rather than a fully independent arithmetic implementation. Overlap is a diagnostic cross-check. The paper identities and rigorous error bounds supply mathematical enclosure validity; overlap alone would not do so.
