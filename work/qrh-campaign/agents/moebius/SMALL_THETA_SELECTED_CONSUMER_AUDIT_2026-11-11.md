## 3. Why the count does not hide an amplitude request

Write C_chi(T) for the true multiplicity count in the full closed strip carrier, including real zeros. The reviewed a.e. identity is

    C_chi(t)=2 theta_(a,c)(t)/pi + S_chi(t)+S_inverse(t).

Its integrated form is

    h C_chi(T)=A + integral(S_chi+S_inverse) - I_aux.

The analytic Turing proof bounds the S integrals; it does not ask the time-array checker to reconstruct a branch of arg L from signs or amplitudes. Each auxiliary sign bracket proves I_aux at least its rational endpoint weight. Therefore C_chi(T)<=U_count follows from separately certified Abar,b and sign-derived W. No interpolation or integration of Y appears in this implication.

Every sign interval gives a critical zero by continuity of the **actual** fixed real normalization, not by identifying roots of U with roots of L. For a nonreal character its inverse's positive intervals supply its negative-ordinate zeros by the source conjugation identity. For a real character the corresponding positive/negative roots are distinct. This proves K<=Ccrit<=C with no simplicity assumption. If an off-line zero exists, rho and 1-conj(rho) are distinct same-character zeros in the same closed-height carrier outside Ccrit. Hence C>=K+2, contradicting the strict test. This also handles off-critical real zeros, without a positivity assertion on the whole real axis.

Default central contribution zero means only a zero lower bound for the central multiplicity. It does **not** assert central nonvanishing or erase that zero from C. Older selected-sign/completeness wording asking for central nonvanishing or exact central data is stronger than the later explicitly selected default. Even if one instead keeps the stricter exact-count U_count<K+1 gate, its soundness does not require the unused central count to be exact: missing critical multiplicity may prevent acceptance, but cannot cause false acceptance. Exact count/moment outputs must nevertheless retain their stronger tagged evidence.

A general complex cluster from `FAMILY_COMPLEX_CLUSTER_COMPLETENESS` is not automatically a valid main contribution here. That older main rule only localizes zeros to Re(s)<=.555 and uses exact saturation. The current original-RH surplus rule needs K<=Ccrit. A narrow box, or even a symmetric box with count two, does not prove all its roots lie on the critical line. Main repairs require a separate critical-line localization/order proof. Auxiliary clusters may count off-line or multiple zeros: their role is only a lower bound for I_aux, with actual counts, ordinate bounds and no double counting. These different uses must have different acceptance tags.
