## 1. Actual source join and what is missing

The raw-head object is fixed by the continued Hurwitz identity at M=1:

    zeta(s,a/c)=(a/c)^(-s)+zeta(s,1+a/c),
    L(s,chi)=c^(-s) sum_(a unit) chi(a) zeta(s,a/c),
    s=1/2+it.

The polynomial evaluates the shifted Hurwitz term. It does not include the first term on the right. The positive character transform is unnormalized. Moving the head to a differently normalized post-transform computation is possible in principle, but requires another arithmetic and error join. It is not the raw-input interface under audit.

## 2. Shared integer-power identity and domain

For positive integers1<=a<c<=265800 and real t, define

    B_x(t)=x^(-1/2)cis(-t log x).

Positive real logarithm and critical-line conjugation give

    c*conj(B_c(t))*B_a(t)
      =sqrt(c/a)cis(t log c-t log a)
      =(a/c)^(-1/2-it).

There is no division by an approximate small complex value. Correlations between the shared power approximations do not affect the error proof below. The sign agrees with the positive-transform raw Hurwitz input. The external factor c^(-s) is still applied by the downstream L stage.

Use the exact coarse time grid t_j=j*(5/64),0<=j<=Jstar, with Jstar<=89600 whenever t_j<=7000. The current guarded sample set is within that domain. A simple conservative producer initializes the entire integer pool1..265800 at j=0 and advances it through the chosen maximum prefix. This is a finite schedule definition, not a new enumeration or a claim that every integer is needed. It requires no factoring or unit-residue scan.

The proof below allows at most90000 recurrence steps, leaving room beyond the required89600 while producing only admitted heights. A shorter requested union may save work only through a separately checked schedule. Late introduction or discarded state requires charged reinitialization; this all-pool baseline uses initialization at zero to avoid any initial cis evaluation.

For negative integer-grid heights, the exact power is the conjugate of its positive-height value. This may supply a head-only conjugate adapter with the same radius. It does not replace the separate inverse-character negative-Y formula, damping enclosure or root-phase convention. Non-grid direct-repair targets require a separate initializer or evaluator; interpolation targets do not automatically extend this coarse-source recurrence.

That is a concrete economical architecture, not a proof of a runtime winner or of lowest end-to-end cost.
