## 5. Matched component comparison

Let alpha and beta price the declared natural-product and natural-adder template events within one resource model. For the main guarded family, full20 to short-final18 leaves a P/A reserve

    10309546483809328*alpha + 4267247894530448*beta.

The corresponding reserve for short-final19 is

    10312052856470600*alpha + 4266299537307264*beta.

A complete comparison must subtract the actual differential conversion, signed/range/shift proof, representation, cache, binding, witness generation and checking costs. The count reductions in other categories are retained, but their widths and prices may differ. No subtraction of two unrelated upper runtime bounds is a measured saving.

Between the two shortened schedules only the final evaluator changes. For main guarded coverage it has U=33,869,900,828 unit outputs. K19/b93 saves 74U=2,506,372,661,272 product events but adds 28U=948,357,223,184 adder events, 6U signed-add bridges, 4U signed-product bridges, 2U nearest implications and an extra output-shift bit per coordinate. It also adds 663,264,800 coordinate conversions under the shared full-layer schedule, and changes the destinations and costs of the retained conversions. Equal counts on a shifted grid are not automatically equal costs.

Thus final19 is cheaper only if its complete remaining differential price is strictly below `74U*alpha-28U*beta`. The same formula with each saved final U applies to all four alternatives. Generation, proof production and checking may assign different prices; none is inferred from the occurrence count alone.
