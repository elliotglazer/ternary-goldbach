## 2. Per-class schedules and the actual query set

Fix one character and one nonzero ell in1..7. Let Q_ell be the set of distinct requested points (k+ell/8)Delta in [0,V], and q=|Q_ell|. Exact duplicates reuse their result at the same precision. Empty classes require no transform or class-specific roots. Let m=N/2 and F(m)=m log2(m)/2.

The half-polyphase route first creates the J positive phased centers, with their negative partners defined by conjugation. It then applies the sparse half packing. Per occupied class its core count is

    G = F(m)+2J                   complex products,
    A = 2F(m)+2J                  complex additions,
    R = 3F(m)+4J+1                complex rounding gates,
    D = 2F(m)                     exact butterfly divisions by two. (2)

The two J product terms are different: one prepares the off-grid phase, the other packs the even/odd real outputs. C_0 has its explicit extra round. Packing also needs 2J conjugations and 2J quarter-turn/coordinate operations, plus zero filling, C_0 duplication and array traffic. These are not general products and are not free data movement.

The normalized half FFT returns N real raw samples packed in N/2 complex outputs. Extraction and exact multiplication by m must be performed for every consumed sample. A persistent full raw service instead shifts and stores all N real outputs once, then answers requests by lookup. The whole class is computed even when q is small. No dependency pruning or partial FFT is claimed.

The local route costs, per distinct query,

    r real products, r-1 real additions, 2r arithmetic roundings,
    r coarse-sample accesses,

plus the target's strict comparison and dependency/provenance work. Weight quantizations are shared setup, not extra arithmetic rounds per query. Its coarse dependency union has at most min(N,rq) elements. If raw coarse samples are not yet materialized, each missing sample's extraction/shift/storage is charged once. A cache-aware comparison must use the actual union, not charge rq distinct samples and later call that an exact saving.

Both routes omit output omega and positive-Y scaling. These operations were already deleted from the sign-only ledger and cannot be deducted again. The initial coarse sign requests remain part of the selected campaign. Their common base transform can be full or half after its own admission; choose the SAME base schedule in both alternatives before canceling it. A different off-grid-only task that never needs the coarse grid has a different common-work baseline.

## 3. Roots, precision, storage and real-product models

The half FFT and packing can reuse the old N-grid root table at sufficient precision; their union is contained in it. Each nonzero class additionally needs phases exp(2*pi*i*j*ell/(8N)), 1<=j<=J. These are independent of the character values and may be cached across compatible rows, but require the strictest accuracy of all actual uses. A full8N root table has at most4N entries; generating only consumed phase roots can use fewer. Neither construction is included in G. A recurrence-generated phase table needs its own error proof and cannot be inferred from the count of required constants.

The first phase products consume the original coefficient centers. Their denominator precision can exceed the time-gate precision. The half-transform report's source-grid a versus gate-grid b bounds remain in force. Later normalized packed nodes have magnitude <=2B plus their proved error. Raw outputs have the much larger bound (2J+1)B plus error, and exact reconstruction shifts both value and error by m. Stencil products use these raw operands and certified real weights. Therefore one dense complex product is not automatically equivalent in bit cost to three or four local real products.

Two explicit exact-arithmetic expansions are useful for comparison:

* Four-product complex multiplication uses ac,bd,ad,bc and two real additions.
* Three-product multiplication uses ac,bd,(a+b)(c+d), then real=ac-bd and imag=(a+b)(c+d)-ac-bd. It uses three real products and five real additions.

For either expansion perform those internal real operations exactly, then apply the ONE complex norm rounding already counted in (2). If internal real operations are rounded separately, a new propagated budget is required. The three-product formula has larger temporary sums and widths; its smaller product count is not a speed theorem.

Under these exact expansions the dense class core has

    real products = mu*G,
    real additions = alpha*G+2A,
    (mu,alpha)=(3,5) or(4,2).                             (3)

Complex rounding remains a separate category. A coordinate implementation may use two appropriately bounded real roundings per complex gate, but their precision and norm conversion must be retained. Exact dyadic divisions, sign/coordinate operations, root certification and data traffic remain outside (3).

Persistent raw storage means N real answers per purchased class, or an explicitly priced persistent normalized packed array supporting exact shifts on demand. One streaming workspace of N/2 complex values does not by itself preserve answers for later arbitrary queries. Buying several classes can require several retained arrays or external storage. Rebuilding an evicted array is additional work. Source coefficients, roots, exact temporaries, proof records and parallel copies remain separate memory categories.

## 5. Priced break-even and a sound online instantiation

For one class, a priced dense purchase K includes (2), roots and packing setup, exact output shifts, writes and persistent storage. A priced local query cost includes its row-specific r products/additions/roundings, actual cache misses, kernel setup on first use, accesses and output checks. All prices are for fixed widths and concrete schedules. Upper-bound prices compare upper-bound ledgers, not measured executions or an optimized unknown alternative.

A convenient instantiation of the reviewed online theorem buys and retains ALL N raw outputs as part of K. Give both methods the same final result-read and strict-sign-check cost d_i. Implement the local schedule by writing its newly computed center to its result cache before that common read/check. Its remaining local cost r_i=l_i-d_i then consists only of nonnegative dot-product, sample-cache, kernel and write costs. This verifies the nonnegative-rent condition for arbitrary nonnegative prices on these specified operations. It does not assume an unproved inequality between arbitrary dense lookup and local arithmetic costs.

The policy accumulates these actual local rents and buys BEFORE the next rent would reach K. It retains all sunk local work. With C common work and D=sum d_i, the accepted prefix proof gives

    ALG <= controller+C+D+2 min(sum r_i,K).               (5)

Thus it is within two of the better fixed all-local or one-purchase dense schedule, apart from the explicit controller term, on the same realized request sequence. Lazy kernel setup and actual sample-cache misses are included in each predicted rent. Repeated queries do not increase the rent. Fixed precision, persistent storage and exact price comparison are required.

A variant retaining normalized dense outputs and shifting only requested samples has a different d_i. It can also use (5), but must separately verify l_i>=d_i. The preceding full-raw-storage construction is a sufficient model that avoids silently assuming that comparison. Its K includes N shifts/stores even if only q answers are requested, so its arithmetic price can be higher.

At fixed r, ignoring all categories except real products gives the diagnostic strict threshold

    q > mu*(F(N/2)+2J)/r.                                (6)

For example N=262144 and r=182 give q>=19490 for mu=3 and q>=25987 for mu=4. Equation (6) is NOT the full purchase threshold. The actual break-even is that the full cumulative local rent exceeds K, including setup, root precision, writes and persistence. The receipt labels every product-only example accordingly. A class may have fewer admissible physical targets than its diagnostic threshold.

Shared setup must be allocated consistently. A fixed initial coarse/root cache can be included once in C. Per-class private prices then give a rigorous comparator, and deduplicating some actual work can reduce the online bill. That does not prove factor two against a globally optimized comparator with cross-class root sharing, shared sample normalization, padded transforms or memory eviction. Different precisions and changed coefficient arrays begin new instances unless a separate incremental reuse proof is supplied. The raw algorithm cannot inherit an earlier T-normalized price model at unchanged widths.
