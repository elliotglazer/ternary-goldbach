A concrete incremental option is to transpose an already valid reusable clause certificate and check a block by OR-ing the columns for its certified anchors. ## 2. Exact source and block interface

Fix the earlier binary geometry B=2E, L>=D+2, D=2^19 and J=L+D. A checked source bit function P on[0,J) satisfies

    P[j]=1 -> Prime(2j+1).

It need not contain every prime. A survivor-based source sieve still needs its complete recursively certified base-prime list; changing the coverage test does not remove that source obligation. Prime2 and source index0 receive their original explicit treatment.

Choose M>=1 distinct palette offsets s_i in[0,D]. For a target tile of origin t0, positive width W and t0+W<=L, define

    H_t={i<M : P[t0+t+s_i]=1}, 0<=t<W.

Consume an accepted compiled family of K>=1 nonempty clauses C_j with its actual-source premises

    C_j=H_(tau_j), tau_j<W,
    for every t<W, some j<K has C_j subset H_t.       (2)

For block r>=1, a sorted distinct list A of a palette indices carries actual primality proofs for

    q(r,s_i)=2rL-(2s_i+1), i in A.

Because L>=D+2, these anchors are at least3. For every target t and a hit i in A intersect H_t, the exact natural-number identity is

    2(rL+t0+t)=q(r,s_i)+[2(t0+t+s_i)+1].             (3)

The source index is at most L+D-1. Thus the original clause theorem proves the tile whenever every C_j intersects A. The equality part of (2) also proves equivalence with direct coverage by the SAME source and palette on that full tile. No new prime is inferred from mask membership without its bound prime fact.

All initial even targets4<=n<2L remain separately certified. Outer blocks are r=1,...,floor(E/L), with final width E-rL+1, including width1 when L divides E. Their tile manifest must be contiguous and complete. A compiled full-tile theorem may soundly restrict to a short prefix, but its full clause test need not be equivalent to direct coverage of that prefix. Use a dedicated prefix certificate or the existing direct fallback, and price the true shorter comparator. No final target or residual exception is dropped.

For the full ternary proof at N0=989*10^24, the separate certified chain still starts at3, has gaps at most B-2 and ends with p_last+B>=N0-1. ## 3. Transpose once, then test the available columns

Define the exact K-bit column for palette index i by

    V_i[j]=1 iff i in C_j, 0<=j<K.                  (4)

Store columns with zero high padding. A new column-binding certificate must prove (4) against the SAME already checked clauses, source object, palette and tile. It can be constructed conservatively by K*M checked bit bindings and packing. No native transpose, hash equality or unchecked array lookup establishes (4).

For a certified availability list A form its exact bitwise union

    U_A = OR_(i in A) V_i.

Then, for every j<K,

    U_A[j]=1 iff some i in A belongs to C_j
                 iff A intersects C_j is nonempty.  (5)

Thus `U_A=ones_K` is equivalent to the prior K-clause condition and, with (2), to direct same-palette coverage on the compiled full tile. The proof is finite Boolean induction over A, followed by the existing clause theorem. It does not rely on an optimal compiler, minimal clauses, all prime anchors, probabilistic fingerprints or small actual K. An empty A fails when K>=1. Duplicate indices could be allowed with their full repeated cost, but the selected schedule checks distinctness before using a as a cardinality.

One ordinary proof implementation processes the actual availability list and maintains a fold theorem for its exact union object. Each word OR uses proved finite-bit semantics. Its final equality uses the correct mask for the last partial word. Every column lookup resolves to its already checked literal or a proved indexed access. After (4) and its universal theorem have been checked, the row cache may be discarded if no other consumer needs it; the theorem statement and all necessary definitions/imports must remain valid. Local proof references do not survive a reset merely because the raw array does.

The old source-cover premise proof is not removed. It is checked once per valid compiled tile and actual proof-environment lifetime. Rebuilding or replaying either that proof or the transpose in separate workers is charged each time. The new circuit supplies no anchor success, so an unavailable or unprofitable template falls back to the existing complete residual/packed/witness checker.

## 4. Typed cost and a concrete break-even

Fix word width w and set b_M=ceil(M/w), b_K=ceil(K/w). Let one prescribed old row scan initialize a word accumulator for each clause, scan all b_M availability words, AND each with the row word and OR into its accumulator, then test nonzero. Its exact Boolean-operation coordinates are

    K*b_M word ANDs, K*b_M word ORs, K nonzero tests.

The proposed fixed column scan initializes b_K union words, scans all b_K words for each of the a admitted indices, then compares with ones_K. Its coordinates are

    a*b_K word ORs, b_K exact-word equalities.        (6)

These are operations on declared finite words, not machine instructions, MMB commands or Lean kernel steps. Bit semantics, word construction, zero/high-padding/range proofs, loops, membership/anchor bindings, reads, theorem applications and serialization have separate prices. An optimized early-exit row schedule must be repriced as its actual schedule; the displayed full scan is not a lower bound on it.

For a fixed illustrative dimension M512,K1024,a16,w64, the row circuit has8192 ANDs,8192 ORs and1024 nonzero tests. The column circuit has256 ORs and16 equalities. Either packed table occupies65536 raw bytes, excluding headers/proofs. Retaining both while checking the transpose requires131072 raw bytes before all other state. The naive transpose certificate has524288 bit bindings. These are dimension examples, not actual source-template sizes or success frequencies.

Let A_w,O_w,N_w,E_w be complete prices for the respective word operations under ONE backend model. Let R_i and C_i include all the remaining per-instance work, at the actual widths and cache states. Then

    Row_i=K*b_M*(A_w+O_w)+K*N_w+R_i,
    Col_i=a_i*b_K*O_w+b_K*E_w+C_i.                  (7)

The existing sparse-witness alternative has its own complete cost Witness_i: K bound membership witnesses and anchor-prime references, with actual lookup, index and source costs. It must not be equated with either K free operations or a full row scan. For an already accepted common compiled dictionary and chosen lifetimes, set D_i to the cost of the selected better row/witness schedule. Selection/binding overhead is included in D_i, not omitted by writing a mathematical minimum.

Let S_trans be the complete incremental cost of column construction, the ordinary transpose proof, its theorem/header representation, loading, cache lifetime and selection policy. For a chosen set I of uses, the exact sufficient guard is

    Col_i<=D_i for i in I,
    S_trans < sum_(i in I)(D_i-Col_i).               (8)

Then replacing only those tests strictly reduces the specified complete work coordinate. Under homogeneous valid reuse with D>Col, the threshold is N>S_trans/(D-Col). If D<=Col, no reuse count pays positive setup. Distinct machines/checkers or cache evictions may multiply S_trans or add reloads; only actual shared checked objects cancel.

For a cold comparison against DIRECT coverage, the old compiled proof is not common. Write C0 for its complete row/cover certificate cost and S_dir for direct setup. The correct inequality is instead

    C0+S_trans+sum selected Col_i+sum fallback Direct_i
       < S_dir+sum all Direct_i,                    (9)

with all differential source/anchor/transport costs included. The direct side may choose the reviewed residual, packed-convolution or explicit-witness terminal, so it need not perform an L-bit scan at every block. Comparing only (6) against a padded upper bound on that side is invalid. If both row and column schedules are available, the complete portfolio includes Direct, Row, Witness and Col, their setup unions and actual lifetimes. It is not the sum of unrelated per-instance minima with all setups free.

Generation, ordinary proof construction and checking require separate versions of (8)-(9). The native producer can transpose a table without a proof, but that does not pay its ordinary certificate. Conversely a successful checker optimization does not refund failed source/anchor searches or the bounded clause-compiler attempts. All compilation attempts retain the existing total cap. Column cost analysis must not trigger an unlimited new search for favorable templates.

## 5. Decision and precise limit

The next useful missing computation is a complete price for finite-binary acceptance with the row/witness/direct interfaces and this exact column alternative. The concrete next design gate is an ordinary theorem-and-word schedule for (4)-(5), including proof-environment reuse and actual source/anchor bindings. It need not repeat the already reviewed binary geometry or invent a new primality criterion.

The go/no-go test is (8) for a genuinely shared dictionary and (9) against the best matched direct route. If the derived ordinary column price is no smaller, stop this option without a prime experiment. If it is smaller only for a bounded K/a/reuse region, record that region and require future actual certified instances to lie in it. No useful clause family or reuse count is established here. Fixed palettes have known arithmetic obstructions; assuming every block shares one successful palette would invalidate this comparison.

This cannot select a globally cheapest B or prove that binary work dominates principal/repair costs. The finite frontier still requires complete Binary(B,L) and an actual certified ladder, while principal and small-family producer outcomes remain unknown. It does supply a new exact representation choice and a complete conditional decision inequality for a potentially repeated unpriced computation. No final hours or Lean/MM0 advantage are asserted.
