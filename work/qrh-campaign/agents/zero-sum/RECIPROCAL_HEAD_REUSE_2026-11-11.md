# Reciprocal bound from the existing height-1500 head

The proposed finite-RH prime-gap route can replace a new reciprocal-sum certificate through 5000 by the already reviewed head counting moment and analytic count envelope. The replacement gives, for every real U>=1500,

\[
 \sum_{0<|\gamma|\le U}\frac{m_\rho}{|\gamma|}
 <\frac{\log^2(U/(2\pi))}{2\pi}+\frac{2}{25}. \tag{1}
\]

This is a new conditional paper implication from existing external evidence and a reviewed analytic theorem. It is not a Lean theorem, a new zero evaluation, or a proof that the separate bound 3.54 through 5000 holds. It costs no new ordinate or sign data. The main high RH certificate remains required by the consumer.

## Exact inputs

Let N(t) count all nontrivial positive-ordinate zeta zeros with analytic multiplicity and closed cutoff. Put A=1500. The accepted head packet proves no zeros through height14 and

\[
 Q_A=\int_{14}^A\frac{N(t)}{t^2}\,dt
 =\sum_{0<\gamma\le A}m_\rho(1/\gamma-1/A)<1.668.
\]

The raw positive reciprocal sum is Q_A+N(A)/A; Q_A alone is not that sum. The selected analytic input, already reviewed independently, is the closed-height bound

\[
 N(t)\le P(t)+R(t),\quad
 P(t)=\frac{t}{2\pi}(\log(t/(2\pi))-1)+\frac78,
 \quad R(t)=5\log t+20,\qquad t\ge1500.
\]

No sharper historical count result is needed. The head certificate counts the whole critical strip, so this step does not require RH or simplicity. Complex conjugation preserves orders and supplies the negative half. Possible real zeros are outside the displayed sum; their exclusion for a later explicit-formula application is a separate source fact.

## Abel calculation and endpoint control

Finite Stieltjes summation, with every atom counted by its order, gives for U>=A

\[
 S_+(U)=Q_A+\frac{N(U)}U+\int_A^U\frac{N(t)}{t^2}\,dt.
\]

Zeros exactly at A or U obey this identity. In particular no endpoint multiplicity is silently halved or bounded by one. Substitution of P+R and elementary integration give

\[
 S_+(U)-\frac{\ell_U^2}{4\pi}
 <1.668+\frac{\ell_A-1}{2\pi}+\frac{7}{8A}
       -\frac{\ell_A^2}{4\pi}
       +\frac{5\log A+25}{A}-\frac5U,
 \qquad \ell_t=\log(t/(2\pi)). \tag{2}
\]

The equivalent combined constant is
`1.668 - ((ell_A-1)^2+1)/(4*pi) + (5*log(A)+207/8)/A`.
The exact value N(A) cancels, so no new terminal count computation is required. Dropping the negative last term makes (2) uniform in U, including all larger production heights.

The companion scalar proof uses
`3.14159<pi<3.14160`, `7.3132<log(1500)<7.3133`, and
`1.8378<log(2*pi)<1.8379`. Hence `5.4753<ell_A<5.4755`.
Replacing the positive and negative terms separately in (2), and using logA<8, gives the rational upper bound for twice its constant

\[
 2\left[1.668+\frac{5.4755-1}{2(3.14159)}
 +\frac7{12000}-\frac{5.4753^2}{4(3.14160)}+\frac{65}{1500}\right]
 =\frac{15228794754209}{197392382880000}<\frac2{25}.
\]

This proves (1). The rational log bounds come from the positive atanh series with a geometric tail after argument reduction; pi comes from Machin's identity and alternating arctan bounds. Only rational scalar arithmetic was run.
