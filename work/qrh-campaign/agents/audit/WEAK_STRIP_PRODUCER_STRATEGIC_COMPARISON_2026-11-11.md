Moreover, its historical weak-strip conclusion does not supply the ordinary RH premise of the current shorter-height consumer assembly. No numerical function, zero, contour or character evaluations, inventory folds, builds, declarations, proof emission or frozen-file changes were made. This note is a planning obstruction, not an impossibility theorem for contour methods or a new route selection.

## What a contour certificate actually replaces

For a nonprincipal primitive character use the actual entire L(s,chi). A complete accepted cover of [b,2]+i[-H,H] by closed rectangles, with strict whole-boundary nonvanishing and winding zero, excludes beta>=b in that domain. This stronger statement implies beta<=b. `Explicit/RectResidue.lean:360,391` requires all closed sides and identifies the contour integral with positive actual analytic orders in the open interior. Closed nonvanishing supplies the seams and endpoints left out of that interior count. Label completeness and source strip membership remain necessary [1–4,19].

The weak conclusion permits beta=b. The reviewed optional deflation adapter requires exact boundary root names, exact location and derivative-vanishing proofs, plus an entire quotient and its zero-free certificate. A box meeting zero is not such evidence. Moving the contour to b+epsilon leaves a gap. Conductor one uses entire zetaF with its removable value; its logarithmic derivative has the separate pole correction [3–4,7–9].

Successful exclusion needs no isolated interior roots, root brackets, simplicity, critical-line phases, or Turing auxiliary lower count. Using raw L can omit the Gamma/Hardy normalization needed only by the sign route. Those are real changes to the kind of evidence produced. They do not remove the following work:

- Complete actual-character/primitive/inverse binding and both signed heights, including real zeros.
- Actual L enclosures and uniform variation bounds over every panel, with exact shared vertices and closed geometry.
- Strict half-plane tests, rational winding calculations and the analytic-to-count adapter.
- Discovery and repair of difficult panels, new off-critical source/rounding contracts, and exact coverage through the required heights.

The current critical-line route instead retains the full sample/head/transform/scaling/phase/interpolation radius, strict sign tests and a canonical count join [17–18]. Its current finite-RH test is h_T*(K+2)-Abar+W-2*Bbar>0. K must be actual critical multiplicity evidence. The strict pair-deficit rule can leave at most one uncounted critical multiplicity, so the older exact-count/simplicity discussion [10–11] must not be imported as a current requirement. Off-critical pairs and unresolved even multiplicities can still defeat a sign-only producer. Neither increasing precision nor knowing a weaker strip guarantees the count surplus.

A weak contour cannot replace K by an arbitrary enclosed cluster count: that proves location inside the cluster, not criticality. Putting the exclusion boundary at beta=1/2 would require handling the allowed critical zeros on that boundary, with the exact divisor or other critical-location evidence. The existing deflation theorem does not manufacture those identities. Thus there is no already-proved contour shortcut to the current RH target.

## The existing efficiency obstruction is explicit

The original strategy [7–9] already says that the smaller output requirement is not a demonstrated numerical saving. Its factorwise batched Hurwitz/character-transform route still produces phi(c) character values at each contour point. An aggregate count of zero proves all factors zero-free by positivity, but does not remove their evaluation or whole-panel evidence. The elementary aggregate Euler series is valid only at Re(s)>1, not on the left boundary .555.

The same strategy quantifies the weakness of a naive product enclosure: relative factor radii rho give product radius at most (1+rho)^n-1. Requiring that disk to avoid zero imposes (1+rho)^n<2. This is a limitation of that enclosure method, not a lower bound for every algorithm. Factorwise winding avoids the conditioning loss but retains the factorwise evidence. No smaller factor count follows from product notation or unproved Galois-orbit reuse.

The direct evaluator later passed an independent convergence review [20–21], superseding the pending-evaluator wording in the frozen slab paper. For sigma>=b>0 on a fixed rectangle with |s|<=B, its source-faithful sufficient bounds are

    |L-S_N| <= C*N^(-b), C=(c+1)*(1+B/b),
    |L'| <= V, V=(c+1)*(1/b+B/b^2).

These follow from `LGrowth.lean:60–176` and finite Abel summation, including its endpoint term. A panel of length ell with midpoint error e has radius e+V*ell/2. A positive minimum boundary modulus proves eventual refinement, but supplies no effective common panel/precision bound. The reviewed c101 example at height near1000 requires N=2^107 for its deliberately coarse tail target10^-12/3. This is an existing exact diagnostic, not a rerun or a lower bound for faster EM/batched evaluators. It already shows why this convergence proof is not a competitive production schedule.

Similarly, the earlier weighted-excess alternative K<=6 did not discharge its statistic through the inspected density estimates [22]. That review excludes those particular comparison bounds only. It provides neither a skipped character family nor a replacement for the current short-height RH input.

## Decision and the smallest unresolved gate

No currently derived weaker-location certificate can be credited with an end-to-end saving on the admitted current schedule. The earlier work settles sound acceptance and conditional convergence; it leaves two distinct gates:

1. Prove a complete consumer implication at a specified weaker profile, including plus, star, base gap and principal paths, or explicitly select the older global-QRH-dependent assembly and charge its different domain and provider work. A new beta allowance must reprove the low-zero estimates and all-scale budget; it cannot inherit the present RH error terms.
2. Supply a competitive actual-function whole-panel producer bound at that profile. It must account for panel counts, precision/conditioning, off-critical source generation, character transforms or a proved aggregate replacement, exact winding and repairs. Output compression alone is insufficient.

For any proposed matched comparison the necessary symbolic inequality is

    new consumer/library work + new contour dependency union + attempted repairs
      < displaced consumer/library work + displaced sample/count dependency union
        + displaced attempted repairs,

under the same backend prices and exact source coverage. No values for those contour terms, or a uniform panel/margin theorem, are supplied by the inspected record. Old critical-line m41 interpolation, grids, coefficients and price ledgers cannot be relabelled as off-critical contour data.

A genuinely new improvement would be a checked aggregate continuation/conditioning estimate on the left boundary, or a quantitative panel bound whose complete cost beats the replaced sample/count work after the consumer gate. Neither is already established here. This note recommends no new sweep or revival of the old toy contour plan. Principal production, finite coverage and ordinary-library/kernel work also remain separate. The final Goldbach statements and their permitted premises are unchanged.
