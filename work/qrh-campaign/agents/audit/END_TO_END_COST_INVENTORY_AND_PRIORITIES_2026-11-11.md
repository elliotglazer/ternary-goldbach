## 1. Define the complete objective before pricing it

For each system s and fixed accepted mathematical route, use a cost vector rather than one scalar:

    C_s = (generation work, proof-production work, checking work,
           proof bytes, witness bytes, peak memory, disk/traffic, critical path).

For each additive work coordinate, partition the bill as

    library + source/formal bridges + nonprincipal pipeline
    + principal/head + binary coverage + prime ladder
    + final assembly + decoding/import/transport not already charged.

The nonprincipal pipeline includes elementary/EM or theta source production, residue evaluation, character transforms, time transforms, normalization, interpolation and strict comparisons, count/completeness proofs, failed work and repairs. Assign every operation once. A butterfly includes its contracted product/addition/rounding schedule; do not then charge that same expansion a second time. A fused producer/checker reports its combined work once. Successful certificates do not refund unsuccessful discovery.

Keep the theorem objective explicit. If the original inclusive BinaryGoldbachInput through4*10^18 is allowed as a premise, binary production is outside that local theorem's bill. A full unconditional ternary proof must instead discharge its finite coverage or use the source-faithful generalized smaller-B reduction and actually prove that smaller binary range. It may not label a smaller prefix as proof of the original input. The analytic threshold candidate N0=989*10^24 still needs its localized source wrapper and final assembly. None is implemented by this inventory.

## 5. Revised research priorities and what would overturn them

1. **Construct a full ordinary-backend price model on shared interfaces.** The residue matrix now supplies enough circuit alternatives; the missing high-value object is a width/precision/cache histogram joined to complete signed multiplication, rational rounding, shifts, source binding and proof-term construction. Include one large transform tile, one narrow Horner unit, one FD cell and one small raw stencil/dense class as symbolic schedules. This can change several quadrillion-scale categories at once. No timing or build is authorized here. Later approved same-output proof slices would calibrate the missing prices. A proof that a new analytic or algebraic circuit removes a whole family would supersede this priority.

2. **Resolve query and coverage distributions rather than assuming dense work.** For the small branch, preserve a cache-aware local/dense policy and derive prices for the actual admitted length/precision pairs. For residues, obtain the jointly weighted distribution of length, required units, normalization class, source height and backend widths; no separate favorable averages. The important test is R*Delta_res against the thresholds above. If a priced fraction of profitable cells is tiny, further FD micro-optimization falls behind character-transform work. If widespread, it may be the largest arithmetic improvement.

3. **Keep principal and finite coverage as independent estimation gates.** Principal RH through1e8 still needs actual sign/critical-support production and one correctly normalized global upper count, plus separate low-head moments. The +2 deficit design avoids local completeness per shard but not failed signs or analytic error floors. Its illustrative250-million-sample direct schedule is neither an actual count nor a cost lower bound. Binary work needs a complete source-bound coverage leaf and parent model; a ladder needs mixed certificates and failed-search allowances. Any one of these complete models can overturn the spectral ranking. They should not wait for every residue bit refinement.

4. **Price proof representation and libraries before a Lean/MM0 comparison.** Reused generic schemas, retained numeral references and ordinary parallel checking can avoid duplicate arithmetic replay. Concrete witnesses, cross-block imports, definitions and completed-obligation checks still cost work and bytes. Stock mm0-c does not implement the proposed parallel/reclaiming transport. Report shared versus repeated library checks, dependency critical path and peak worker memory. An MM0 arithmetic advantage is not an end-to-end advantage if the required analytic library/bridge is missing; Lean's existing library likewise needs compatible checking and source-faithful assembly. These are unpriced terms, not zeros or recursively mandated re-audits of all mathematics.

The revised priority is therefore complete priced composition and success/coverage sensitivity, not an endless sequence of local bit savings. Keep research on a new centered spectral estimate as a separate high-risk option: its potential family deletion can exceed all circuit improvements, but no conjectural residual bound enters this bill. No ranking here survives arbitrary unknown principal, binary, repair or library costs; the displayed thresholds identify the evidence that would change it.
