## 1. Common boundary that every implementation must preserve

Keep three coefficient objects distinct. First are the true scaled coarse jets of f(alpha)=zeta(s,1+alpha), s=1/2+it, at the named coarse anchor. Second is the ideal FINITE translated coefficient vector obtained from the retained coarse jets by the pinned translation and normalization formula. It is not silently identified with the vector of true fine-anchor jets. Third are the stored rounded centers z_j delivered for P(h)=sum z_j h^j. The producer bounds numerical error to that finite translated vector; the analytic shift bias and target-tail remainder attach the finite polynomial to the actual function separately. This blueprint requires exactly that inherited split, not a stronger per-coefficient enclosure of the true fine jets.

The evaluator starts with the ORIGINAL delivered-center polynomial P and charges any new grid conversion relative to those z_j. The output displacement is h=D_out(alpha-anchor). The final joint seed uses source D4096 but output D_out8192. A state must bind the delivered normalization, not substitute the source mesh. Original EM, elementary-leaf, coarse-jet/coefficient numerical error, finite-translation numerical error, analytic shift bias and target-tail errors remain outside the evaluation allowance.

At a required residue alpha=a/c, set F=2D_out, v=2c and r=Fa-kc for anchor k/F. The selected ties-down cell gives |r/v|<=1/4. Record its exact endpoint convention. A center-accuracy bound does not prove that centers lie on any common dyadic grid. Arbitrary finer source encodings retain their decoding and conversion costs. The converted coefficient norm bound C=2^16 must follow from the source's quantitative margin; it is not an added final hypothesis.

Every method returns a complex dyadic center on grid100, with its own certified error against P(h). Let u=2^-100. The old evaluation allowance is 8u/3 per residue. Under the pinned source's absolute character/conductor amplification633 this gives1688u. An arbitrary mix of admitted methods retains that cap. An all-method subtotal, such as FD's1477u, does not become the bound for a mixed schedule. The methods need not return identical centers or certify the same strict signs.

This boundary only replaces finite polynomial evaluation. Finite-head corrections, the source Hurwitz-to-L identity, c^-s, actual character labels, both relevant channels, subsequent transforms and phase/sign/count consumers remain required. No analytic assertion is made at the extra polynomial support points used in full-state FD.

## 4. Normalization and initialization records

For exact FD write c=2^s*w with w odd, F=2^f, and t=min(f,s+nu2(k),s+1), using the separate even-anchor rule for k=0. Record exact divisibility and reduced r',v',F'=2^delta. Partial Newton degree i cancels2^(it), not the completed degree19 factor from every prescale. For power-only order j use lambda_j=j*delta+nu2(j!). Direct integer recurrences prove the states; repeated factorial division is not an assumed operation.

A required unit output has an additional2^19 numerator/denominator cancellation when s=f and k is odd. Record this as an output-only adapter. The intervening nonunits need not have that factor. Horner is entitled to the same per-target fraction reduction and any proved alignment cancellation. An odd-core product proof followed by a shift does not permit dropping dividend bits or narrowing unchecked residuals.

The conservative rounded-init row consumes exact d_j=I_j/(2^100*v^19). Its initial round proves X=I_j*2^(p-100), denominator v^19 and the nearest witness above. All40 coordinate witnesses are present. A new price paper proposes using the normalized initializer with X_j=E_j*2^(p-100+lambda_j) and subsequent exact power cancellation. That adapter and its complete prices are a separate composition gate in this snapshot. Until accepted, use the reviewed initializer directly and do not cancel its cost against a different initializer.

## 6. Complete prices and implementation order

For each selected cell-set, price coefficient conversion by actual retained objects, geometry and power setup, initialization, every numerator advance, required exports, witness generation, ordinary proof construction/checking, term/reference bytes, cache traffic and peak coexistence. State capacities bound successful operands; untrusted records need bounded parsing before arithmetic. Include failed-policy exploration if it is performed. A metadata policy may avoid that work only if it decides before constructing the rejected state.

Uniform, linear and optimized Horner retain nineteen complex rounded products and nineteen exact additions. Under the improved accepted unsigned-byte template their product subtotals are17632,16252 and15442. The older16312 linear subtotal uses a different sufficient width envelope and remains a conservative comparator, not an additional saving. None includes full rounding, signs, alignment, conversion or setup. The optimized vector has seven nonzero shifts totaling29 bits; uniform has none. Different arithmetic backends change the objective.

Power-only exact Newton retains190 general complex products,357 additions,357 shifted/reference operands and19 coefficient prescales. Each advance has19 shifted additions. The full-factorial variant has its own190 initialization additions/top copies and40 additions/40 shifted operands per advance under the declared binary schedule. Copies, widths and differing setup prevent an equal-addition subtotal from choosing a winner.

Rounded-init adds20 wide complex conversions to its exact initializer, then19(L-1) narrow complex additions and U final dyadic conversions. Its complete comparison against exact FD must pay these conversions and any initializer/format difference. Compare complete declared schedule prices separately for production, proof generation and checking, or a new upper bound against an old lower bound. Subtracting unrelated upper bounds is not a demonstrated saving. Shared setup can couple cell decisions, so per-cell minima need not give a global minimum under cache or memory limits.

A minimal implementation sequence is: prove shared signed arithmetic/rounding and source-object semantics; prove one original or uniform Horner checker and coverage join; instantiate closed conversions and arithmetic records; then add alternative grid vectors and the two FD families through the same output interface. The sequence is a handoff recommendation, not an execution authorization. No concrete coefficient data or successful signs have been supplied here.
