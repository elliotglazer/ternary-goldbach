## Exact factor and width interpretation

    692*f <= 2^(d+9),
    equivalently f <= (128/173)*2^d.

The non-strict boundary is essential. At equality the exponent is d+9. This argument also covers negative d; it is an identity about positive rational factors rather than an assumption that all factors exceed one. The old ceil class cannot determine which side of this internal threshold f occupies. No exact width distribution follows by subtracting five magnitude bits from every class.
