## Weighted character panel

The panel fixes two draws in each of seven conductor-band and modulus-parity strata. Conductors are sampled with weights proportional to the number of primitive nonreal labels times the number of physical grid intervals. Each draw checks a primitive inverse pair. The selection encompasses 14 draws; it uses the preexisting structural manifest as the seed. Its frozen SHA-256 is `343e001fb602519fa59b487928ded5972a3e700475a0cb36a99dba97cde2f43e`.

At the archive snapshot, **12 of 14 draws** had successful per-draw exit receipts. All 12 passed the native moderate-allowance count test with a 50-unit guard at the aligned main endpoint. They contained **119 proposed interior repairs across 416,804 character intervals**. All 119 passed the short interpolation test using 12 nodes and 16-bit sample heads. The largest observed moderate excess (U-K) was 0.964435 at conductor 126,751; the passing threshold is strictly below 1. The twelve completed draws use native computations conditional on the analytic enclosures and allowance.
