The exponent equality `-s-1=-(s+1)` is proved algebraically. Continuity on `[1,N]` and compact-interval integrability are proved at :30–36. The library Abel theorem handles the step-function partial sums; its weighted-integrand integrability mechanism is visible in `AbelSummation.lean:91–125`.
