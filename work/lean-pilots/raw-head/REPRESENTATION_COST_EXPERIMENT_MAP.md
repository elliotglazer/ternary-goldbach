# What the computed raw-head block can measure

13 November 2026 UTC. Read-only source and accounting audit. This note changes no forecast, source program, fixture, proof or benchmark. No model script, generator, evaluator or build was executed. Line references below are repository-relative and refer to the pinned snapshots at the end.

The proposed eight-base, 128-step experiment can measure complete repeated raw-state arithmetic and its proof/output packaging under one matched analytic contract. It cannot, by itself, calibrate or remove the model's largest HOL planning row. That row estimates fresh **input-to-evaluator translation for spectral payloads**, not one certificate for every raw-state update. The experiment also deliberately retains the whole literal trace, so it cannot claim to have eliminated the output boundary.

## 1. What the 227.54-core-year row actually counts

`forecast-audit-20261113/end_to_end_model.py:70`–`:77` sets the backend anchors, scenario allowances and this accounting denominator:

    N = ceil((920122241148146 + 89017934196032
              + 218799609396458) / 8192).

These are large root products, large kernel products, and small character/time butterflies. The resulting saved count is 149,894,993,255 notional blocks. It does **not** count raw integer recurrence updates, raw residue-head products, EM steps, or initializer objects. It is not a certified partition of any actual dataflow graph.

At script lines119–122, the planning calculation is

    retained = ceil(N * 1.02 * 1.1),
    literal_representation = retained * 4.269485 / 100 / 31557600.

The saved JSON's HOL4 planning category is 227.5367281304526 core-years. This is an existing forecast number, quoted without recomputing a new forecast. The factors mean one fresh fixture-sized translation per 100 retained notional blocks, a 1.02 auxiliary allowance, and a 1.1 acceptance-refinement allowance. Neither the number100 nor the mapping from mathematical operations to payload lifetimes was measured. `END_TO_END_MODEL.md:99`–`:103` says so explicitly. The .073960-second runtime anchor is a different measurement and does not enter this row.

The translation fixture contains 128 complex input pairs and 64 complex coefficient pairs, or192 objects. `hol4-setup/generate_dependent.py:92`–`:109` builds named conservative definitions and calls `cv_trans` on them. Crucially, `hol4-setup/summarize_dependent.py:111`–`:113` defines the 4.269485-second statistic as the median of **translate_block_inputs plus translate_block_coefficients only**. It excludes the separately timed `construct_inputs` and `construct_coefficients`, the scale translation, source calculation, semantic enclosure proofs and full process/export work. The model uses that measured subphase as a payload-translation proxy; it is not a measurement of every representation task. Future replacements must expose those omitted construction/binding roles instead of calling the proxy a complete measured setup price.

The earlier HOL block already computes all successive butterfly outputs internally. It does not import a supplied output at every arithmetic gate (`hol4-setup/DEPENDENT_BLOCK_REPORT.md:25`–`:31`). Consequently, moving raw recurrence intermediates inside a verified loop does not identify a new refund for the old HOL internal intermediates: they were never in its input-translation anchor. The remaining question is whether **new transform inputs and coefficient payloads** can reach their consumers without repeated literal-to-evaluator translation.

The current model deliberately charges separate cache admission at script line125, using `(363202368 + 24654496) * setup / 192` and reload factors1/3/10. It also retains the whole192-object runtime-payload proxy even where cache sharing could eliminate coefficient retranslations (`END_TO_END_MODEL.md:89`). This is a known conservative overlap in representation roles, not an audited duplicate amount available for immediate subtraction. An actual object/lifetime map is needed to identify which coefficient objects are charged twice and whether their checked representations survive to the consumer. Dividing the192-object anchor into a uniform per-object rate is itself a model assumption; input and coefficient translations need separate measurements.

All measured replacement scopes must include ordinary proof acceptance. An expression naming a computed fold is not a usable accepted output unless its concrete interpretation and consumer binding are proved. Conversely, a large printed numeral is not necessary at every internal gate if a checked executable semantics supplies exactly that value to the next gate. The experiment is designed to distinguish these implementations while preserving the same externally visible trace.

## 3. What can disappear, and what only moves

There are three distinct boundaries.

1. **Inside an accepted loop.** Per-operation literal witnesses, separate theorem declarations and repeated source naming may be avoidable. Their computation, rounding semantics and dependent propagation remain. The computed loop may replace proof construction with evaluator reduction and global soundness application. Measure all three; fewer declarations alone was not faster in the finite Lean replay.
2. **At the sample's trace export.** The full1032-pair literal trace is mandatory in both variants. Its proposal generation, decoding, coordinate equality, shape/index checks and concrete output theorem cannot be refunded. Internal execution may avoid1024 separately represented update proofs, but cannot silently downgrade the output to existence, a bound or a checksum. The older HOL network uses an existential vector plus checksum, so its .073960-second acceptance is not a same-output-contract benchmark for this experiment.
3. **Between future source and spectral consumers.** A consumer could accept the actual output of the checked source program directly rather than reimport all its coordinates as fresh independent literals. This can avoid an input translation only if the source-to-consumer representation is retained, the exact object/index/grid/rounding identity is established, and the consumer runs against it. Materializing the same array as fresh theorem-language literals and translating it again merely relocates the cost. Retaining a native array or hash without the semantic binding does not qualify. Cross-shard persistence needs explicit state identity, accumulated error, loading trust and reconstruction costs.

The current sample proves no such complete source-to-transform handoff. It stops at raw integer-state traces. The dominant spectral inputs also require raw conductor factors and residue products, shifted-Hurwitz coefficients and contraction, source assembly/regridding, and actual transform coefficients. Its eight fixed steps are not a certified general unit-root catalogue or Bluestein kernel cache. The plan correctly stops before those claims.

Internal-only production might remove some external numerical preparation, but only after an implemented consumer no longer needs the independent literal trace proposal. Under the current matched benchmark it still does. Internalizing source generation also adds accepted computation: do not remove both the external generation and the source-production row while counting only the final generic theorem application.

## 4. Highest-value measurements and bookkeeping

The largest remaining uncertainty is the **number and lifetime of fresh semantically checked payloads at actual source/consumer and shard boundaries**, followed by the cost of accepting their arithmetic with the real widths and complete output contracts. Initializer speed alone addresses neither.

For the already scoped experiment, keep the following measurements separate, as required at `COMPUTED_BLOCK_EXPERIMENT.md:52`–`:73`:

- Generic program, integer-rounding and analytic soundness library construction.
- Eight seed/step proposals; literal term construction; source acceptance; shared-cache setup.
- Untrusted full-trace/witness construction and its actual bytes.
- Concrete proof construction and first admission, including the exact trace equality.
- Complete renamed concrete-closure kernel replay. Record imported shared support separately; do not filter out numerical helpers by namespace.
- Input/output binding CPU, output bytes, process CPU/elapsed, peak memory and live-object lifetime.

The source enclosures and initializer cost are common only when the exact objects and lifetime are common. First-admission totals should charge them once per actual lifetime. Replays are diagnostic replications, not additional production work. If construction already includes first checking, do not add a standalone full replay to the default construction total.

A future representation calibration, requiring a separate decision, must establish an actual source-to-consumer block rather than infer it from1024 raw updates. Record at least: external inputs; internal source outputs; coefficient-cache identities; actual consumers; fresh translations by role; retained/reloaded objects; exact boundary exports; and any recomputation after release. Keep original-input construction and evaluator translation as distinct timers. This replaces the unknown reuse100 by observed lifetimes for the chosen graph; it does not extrapolate that lifetime to unrelated conductors or time FFTs. Root/chirp and data payloads need separate measurements because their producer and reuse patterns differ.

A compact accounting rule is to replace only the sum of old categories assigned to the **same identified role and lifetime**, with the full measured replacement cost:

    source production + external proposal + representation/binding
      + first acceptance + required output/export + reload/reconstruction.

Every retained role receives one owner. If new internal production consumes already accepted source objects, charge their earlier construction once; if it recreates them, charge the recreation. If retaining a representation eliminates a translation, retain its memory/import cost. If cache translation was already separately charged, remove a runtime-payload coefficient allowance only for proved shared objects, not a second time from the cache row. If fewer serialized bytes are demonstrated, update the corresponding byte and serialization role together, not the separate computational acceptance twice. I/O wall time is a separate bandwidth constraint, not extra CPU work.

## 5. Untouched obligations and stopping condition

No result of this sample alone prices the60,254,515,577,760 raw residue assemblies,1,528,006,797 conductor caches, full20 Horner contractions, EM head/correction/shift/height producers, actual root and kernel construction, small-family wide time transforms, source-to-transform conversion, completed L-functions, canonical phases, sign repairs, zero counts, principal verification, finite binary coverage or the dense prime ladder. Their rows remain in the model. Shared mathematical identities may later support another implementation, but shared identities do not make measured operation costs interchangeable.

The ordinary-PA MM0 scalar-production scenario at model lines146–171 also remains unchanged. A successful Lean loop is neither an ordinary-PA executable proof nor evidence of a faster MM0 producer. HOL4 and Candle need their own implementations and full source/error/consumer joins. No universal language factor or foundation transfer follows.

Decision after the proposed matched experiment: report whether complete first admission and complete output binding became cheaper, separately from replay; identify the measured mechanism and any resource failure. Do not issue an end-to-end discount. The decisive representation question remains open until an actual consumer uses the checked internal source without rebuilding its literal payload. This note proposes no additional run or enlarged experiment.
