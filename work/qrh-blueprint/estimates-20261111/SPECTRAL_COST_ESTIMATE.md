## 7. Interpretation, limits and what could change the estimate

These figures are conditional CPU work, not wall time. Dividing by machine cores is only a lower bound before dependency depth, memory, disk/network, scheduling and proof-context limits. A thousand nominal cores do not prove thousandfold effective throughput. Serialized emission and checking add CPU work even if pipelined.

The reviewed mean186 assignment has49750 node/p states,9052 kernels and369417568 entries, with p74..99. It improves every predecessor precision by five or six bits but increases its cold kernel entries by6215200. No exact runtime-price fold exists for V_width692. This estimate leaves the accepted combined8828 price unchanged in every scenario. A future exact-price result should replace that slot only, retaining source, raw head, converter, setup and postprocessing bills.

The largest uncertainty is not the last few bits: it is whether the ordinary proof implementation can amortize or avoid enormous local arithmetic/proof traffic while retaining exact source and acceptance semantics. Small wide transforms, requested repair density and formal arithmetic compilation can change the total by orders of magnitude. Changing the certificate representation or mathematical evaluation method can invalidate this particular workload estimate in either direction; no optimality claim is made.

The final theorem must use standard axioms with all actual finite certificates checked. Paper reviews and saved metadata are not Lean/MM0 proofs. Existing Lean wrappers still need the source-faithful finite-RH localization; analytic completion, exact arithmetic templates, source encodings, cached-object identities, strict signs, closed-zero/multiplicity completeness and final joins remain. Ordinary PA arithmetic alone does not supply complex analysis, and the full MM0 analytic translation is not implemented. If those bridges or required spectral evidence cannot be completed, no finite completion estimate applies.

The spectral estimate therefore returns a complete partition with explicit provisional allowances and a wide sensitivity range, rather than zeroing unfinished work. The separate finite estimate must be added once by the coordinator. No principal extension, extra reciprocal-head obligation, binary/ladder term or speculative global spectral reuse is added twice here.
