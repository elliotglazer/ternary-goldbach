## 4. Representative complete certificates

### Positive-height spectral pilot

The pilot must begin with an actual Dirichlet character and positive-height spectral input, prove the analytic finite approximation and tail, check the rounded numerical calculation, and connect it to the actual analytically continued L-function and its Hardy normalization/sign. The intended small fixture is the character modulo four at height 5/64. The exact normalization and exported theorem must be reviewed from the real definitions, with no hidden enclosure, tail, source-identity or phase premises.

A central-point positivity result alone does not satisfy this requirement. Neither does a standalone complex multiplication, a rounding identity, or a theorem conditional on the desired analytic enclosure. Those can be internal milestones only. Completion needs the full semantic chain, ordinary axioms, a negative control and a measured complete local certificate. A tiny conductor cannot by itself validate the production large-transform throughput assumptions.
