For illustration of compatible operation categories, if every G gate uses the fixed-coefficient three-product identity, the runtime real counts are 3G products and 2A+3G additions, plus coefficient setup. Compared with the original R150000 whole-axis baseline (4G_old products and 2A_old+2G_old additions), the exact ratios computed by the receipt are about 0.4197009 and 0.5982277. These are NOT runtime ratios or full-proof ratios. They assume the quoted inventories, exclude setup and all other stages, and compare operations whose widths and proof costs can differ. The mathematical compatibility is useful: the improvements need not invalidate each other's error arguments. The numeric ratios are not sufficient to pick an implementation. In particular neither real-gate ratio scales seed generation, input contraction, interpolation, Turing completeness, source proofs, root generation, libraries or theorem assembly.

## Binary coverage: reusable transforms with bounded caches

The NTT grid-reuse author/review pair passes at exact geometry and conditional proof-reuse scope. With one actual fixed anchor histogram per block and nominal source vector per tile, the full grid needs J+R+C transforms rather than 3C, plus CT point products. Final short cells reuse the nominal source vector but consume only their actual target indices. Every coefficient/frequency remains checked. Partially successful passes preserve the exact residual mask.

At the broad-halo example B=1e16, L=2^32, W=2^18 and T=2^21, retaining 64 source transforms gives a tile-strip product ratio

    85187496783561061 / 235999808732452143,

between .3609642 and .3609644. Its 512 MiB is raw retained transform outputs only. Coefficient buffers, source and anchor facts, root tables, proof objects, residuals and indexing add memory and work. A fresh checker context must replay required proofs or use a valid preserved checked environment; hashes cannot replace theorem proofs.

This does not establish NTT as the best binary method. The earlier narrow-span isolated transform already uses fewer products per cell than the broad-envelope fully shared limit. Narrow-span transforms and residual word-OR can also share appropriate inputs. Actual source arrays, anchor success, fallback and the initial finite interval remain unproved here.
