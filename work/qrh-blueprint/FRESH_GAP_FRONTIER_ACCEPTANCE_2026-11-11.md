## Exact conditional consequence

For 1<=Q<=50, define H=4*10^10*Q and a=.031+2.261/Q. Complete finite RH through H, the height-1500 head/count inputs and the named Logan analytic lemmas imply

    |theta(x)-x| < a*sqrt(x)*log(x)^2
    for 10^16<=x<=10^27.

This is ordinary theta. A prime-power endpoint jump is paid explicitly. The three zero bands cover their exact domains with multiplicities; the high band uses an unconditional tail estimate. The proof avoids the published theorem's 3*10^12 initialization, separate height-5000 data and the sharp historical inverse-square sum. A weaker remainder below 3 follows from the existing centered head and analytic count.

Let U=125539470000000000. Any even B>=2*10^16 with 2*a*U<B-4 supplies the finite ternary range through 10^27 from binary Goldbach through B. Bertrand's theorem covers the low transition without a stored prime or explicit ladder. The smallest integer Q reaching the conservative binary floor is 47, with H=1.88*10^12 and B=2*10^16. Q=46 does not satisfy this floor test. These are sufficient allocation choices, not lower bounds on what mathematics could achieve.

Its scaling, hardware and formal-checking factors remain uncalibrated. The new gap family stays in the comparison set; it does not replace the explicit-ladder baseline by default.
