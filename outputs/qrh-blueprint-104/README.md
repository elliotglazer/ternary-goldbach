## One consistent numerical target

Here `x>=4.9e26`. The cubic loss, major/minor contributions, nonprime subtraction, and final reserve are coefficients of `x^2/49`. The energy `J` is the source major L2 integral divided by `x`; the residuals are dimensionless twist-domain integrals defined in chapter 03.

|---|---:|---|
| Lower major energy | `J>=8.6296` | Reviewed consequence of full-family Ep; finite spectral input unproved |
| Upper major energy | `J<=10` | Reviewed finite-strip implication; finite spectral input unproved |
| Coprime plus residual | `Aplus'<=5e-7` | Reviewed finite-strip implication; analytic bridges unformalized |
| Coprime star and mixed residual | `Astar'+Cross'<=9.5e-7` | Reviewed fourth-moment implication; finite spectral input unproved |

| Nonprime subtraction | `<.00271` | Reviewed numerical route; revised Lean proof open |
| Final positive reserve | `.00009` | Exact arithmetic |

The dynamic loss is `sqrt((2J+17.41053338496)*Aplus)+sqrt(2J*(Astar+Cross))`, using full residuals after the coprime-removal corrections. The `.011670451` budget in earlier exploratory notes omitted the nonprime subtraction and is superseded. The variable-J alternative in the averages chapter has different residual caps and must be treated as a separate complete package.
