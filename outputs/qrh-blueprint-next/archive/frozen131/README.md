# QRH replacement research blueprint

Read the [current strategy](chapters/70-current-strategy.md) for the selected proof route. This package records research by the eight rotating agents and the coordinator. It is not a completed Lean proof. The large theorem must still start at `10^27` with no analytic premise; the full theorem retains exactly `BinaryGoldbachInput` through `4*10^18`.

The earlier, independently reviewed 104-chapter checkpoint is preserved in `outputs/qrh-blueprint-104`. Earlier chapters in this archive describe historical proposals and superseded constants. The current strategy, the selected contracts below and their dated reviews determine what the present route uses.

## Selected route

The global seven-eighths QRH theorem supplies the infinite-height input. The finite family target is now

    beta <= 111/200 through Hcert(c) = min(Hp_old(c), 100000000/c),

for every actual nontrivial zero of every active primitive character, including conductor one and conjugates, with inclusive heights and true multiplicities. The [new major-tail argument](chapters/120-common-height-major.md) passed [independent review](chapters/121-common-height-review.md). It uses a full mixed remainder norm below `.000160001`; the old `2e-10` value does not apply to this profile. The finite assertion remains unproved. Its heights fit within the common published Platt domain, but no published computation has been imported as a proof.

The [complete conditional base-gap join](chapters/101-full-base-gap-join-review.md) covers all 150,000 reduced denominators at every required twist and scale. Its dynamic heights fit both the old profile and the common `1e8/c` height, hence their minimum. The [height and contour-certificate review](chapters/112-spectral-strategy-review.md) records that argument. The [erratum](chapters/111-spectral-strategy-erratum.md) supplies the required conductor-one logarithmic-derivative correction. The parity, actual etaU smoothing, weighted character and Type I/II producer bridges still need proofs; coverage alone does not discharge them.

The finite prime-error interval is now `[1e9,5e9]`. Every one of its 6,439 windows has concrete candidate data, and an [independent full recount](chapters/124-full-prime-recount-review.md) matched all 125,271 subcounts and every original hash. The [checkpoint repair](chapters/117-stress-repair-review.md) uses `|theta(a)-a|<=a/10000` on the unchanged grid, preserving the required `|psiN(v)-v|<=.01*v/log(v)`. There are zero uncounted windows and zero new Lean count proofs. The [full data handoff](chapters/122-full-prime-candidate.md) and [protocol review](chapters/123-full-prime-protocol-review.md) distinguish exact arithmetic, file integrity, and actual recounts.

The analytic psi tail from `5e9` still needs additional low-zeta information through height `1e6`. The sufficient construction uses a strip `(.499,.501)`, a positive-ordinate reciprocal head `Q1500<=2`, and an analytic count bound. The [new count proof](chapters/127-analytic-zeta-count.md), with [independent review](chapters/128-analytic-zeta-count-review.md), gives `N(t)<=F(t)+5*log(t)+20` for `t>=1500` without historical finite zero data. Its resulting weighted moment is below `23.990<24`. The [height-14 exclusion](chapters/129-zeta14-certificate.md) has a complete external interval certificate and [independent review](chapters/130-zeta14-review.md). The narrow strip and complete head certificate remain outstanding, as does formal replay of the height-14 result.

The [conductor-three contour pilot](chapters/125-c3-contour-pilot.md), independently [reviewed](chapters/126-c3-contour-review.md), validates a complete `.555` rectangle through height 33. It supports requested height 32 only, far below the production height. No whole-family finite spectral certificate exists.

## Numerical assembly and remaining work

| Contribution | Selected bound, in units of `x^2/49` |
|---|---:|
| Major arcs | at least 1.0404 |
| Absolute minor arcs | at most 1.0376 |
| Nonprime subtraction | less than .00271 |
| Positive reserve | .00009 |

The unchanged coprime targets are `Aplus<=5e-7`, `Astar+Cross<=9.5e-7`, `N<=1.23`, `Ep<=1e-4`, and `8.6296<J<=10`. The new common-height calculation preserves them. The exact final budget checker verifies their numerical implication, not the analytic hypotheses.

Remaining work includes the complete finite spectrum, the low-zeta narrow strip and head counts, local validation of the genuine QRH provider, analytic/formal bridges, kernel replay of the prime and interval certificates, and final consumer/type/axiom audits. The [base-gap interface ledger](chapters/106-base-gap-interface-ledger.md) and its [review](chapters/107-base-gap-interface-review.md) distinguish actual source contracts from unavailable RH-specific wrappers. The reviewed elementary P2/P3/P6, totient, quotient-sieve and Möbius repairs remain part of the selected route.

No new Lean build or canonical theorem edit occurred in this research phase. Three helper declarations retain earlier check evidence; no new Goldbach theorem is checked. The older `exp(35)` psi tail and much longer finite gap remain a separate alternative. Weighted and other height profiles cannot be mixed with the current remainder ledger without a fresh proof.

The package includes Markdown, HTML, a LaTeX research outline, copied external evidence, and a coarse acyclic research map. The map is not an author-approved proof dependency database. Manifest and source-copy checks establish packaging integrity only. No rendered leanblueprint, `checkdecls`, final root compilation or transitive axiom audit is claimed.
